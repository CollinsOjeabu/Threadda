Is this just ChatGPT with a nicer editor?+

No. A general model writes from your prompt and starts from zero every time. Threadda starts from the full source — the study, the report, the thread — and from a model of your published voice. The angle is chosen before a single word of draft is written.

Do I have to hand over my whole posting history?+

No. A handful of posts you're proud of is enough to start. The more you publish through Threadda, the closer the voice gets.

Which platforms does it write for?+

LinkedIn and X — each with its own voice and format, not one draft reflowed into two boxes.

What can I put in?+

An article URL, a PDF, or notes of your own. Threadda reads the whole thing, not the first three paragraphs.

Will the draft sound like me?+

That's the whole point. Every draft starts from your voiceprint — your rhythm, your stance, the words you use and the ones you don't — then you edit from there.
